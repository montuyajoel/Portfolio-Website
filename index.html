My career began with a foundation in Computer Engineering and an interest in understanding how software, hardware, and systems work together. I moved from embedded systems and microcontrollers into backend engineering and applied AI, gaining 2.5 years of commercial experience at Accenture Philippines developing enterprise conversational AI applications, API integrations, security workflows, production deployments, and support across 13 live chatbot use cases. I later expanded my technical foundation through an MSc in Data Analytics and a Diploma in DevOps, strengthening my capabilities in machine learning, deep learning, cloud infrastructure, CI/CD, Docker, and Kubernetes. More recently, I have focused on building Python and FastAPI services, LangChain and LangGraph agents, RAG applications, and cloud-deployed backend systems. My current direction is toward Applied AI and Backend Engineering roles where I can combine production software engineering, AI integration, and scalable infrastructure.
